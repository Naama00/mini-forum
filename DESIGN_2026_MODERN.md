## ✨ Visual Effects

### Glow Effects
- **Neon Glow**: Dynamic box-shadows that pulse and intensify on hover
- **Text Glow**: Colored text-shadows with animated intensity
- **Border Glow**: Gradient borders that emit light
- **Inset Glow**: Internal glowing effects within cards and containers

### Animations
- **Fade In Down** (`fadeInDown`): Elements fade and slide down on load
- **Slide In Up** (`slideInUp`): Cards and items slide up with opacity
- **Scale In Up** (`scaleInUp`): Elements grow from center on appearance
- **Float Up** (`floatUp`): Continuous gentle floating motion
- **Color Shift** (`colorShift`): Smooth color transitions and pulsing
- **Gradient Shift** (`gradientShift`): Background gradients animate subtly
- **Progress Pulse** (`progressPulse`): Progress bars emit glowing pulses
- **Neon Glow** (`neonGlow`): Cards emit pulsing neon borders

### Hover Effects
- **Card Lift**: `translateY(-8px) scale(1.02)` with enhanced shadow
- **Button Shine**: Gradient shine effect sweeps across on hover
- **Color Intensity**: Colors brighten and glow increases
- **Border Highlight**: Borders shift to accent color with glow
- **Icon Scale**: Icons grow and emit glow on hover

## 📐 Responsive Design

### Breakpoints
- **Large (1024px+)**: Full desktop experience
- **Tablets (768px-1024px)**: Adjusted spacing and font sizes
- **Mobile (480px-768px)**: Single column layouts, reduced padding
- **Small (< 480px)**: Minimal padding, optimized for touch

### Responsive Adjustments
- Modal width reduces for mobile
- Grid columns stack on small screens
- Navigation adapts to screen size
- Buttons full-width on mobile
- Font sizes use clamp() for fluid scaling

## 📊 Animation Timing

- **Fast** (0.15-0.25s): Instant feedback, micro-interactions
- **Normal** (0.3-0.4s): Smooth transitions, hover effects
- **Slow** (1-4s): Background animations, looping effects

### Cubic Bezier Curves
- **Smooth**: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` - Natural motion
- **Sharp**: `cubic-bezier(0.6, 0, 0.4, 1)` - Quick response
- **Bounce**: `ease-out` - Slightly over-animate then settle
